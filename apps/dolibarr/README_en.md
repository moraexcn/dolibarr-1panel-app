## Introduction

**Dolibarr** is an open-source ERP and CRM web application for businesses, foundations, and freelancers, bringing customer, supplier, sales, finance, and operations management together.

## Features

- **Customer and Supplier Management**: Manage customers, suppliers, contacts, and related business information.
- **Sales and Finance Workflows**: Handle proposals, orders, invoices, payments, and accounting workflows.
- **Operations Management**: Cover stock, agenda, HR, expense reports, documents, and manufacturing.
- **Modular Extensions**: Enable built-in features and install external modules as needed.
